# Visual QA: Phase 3, 2026-10-22

**Workstream:** P5 (Playwright E2E + visual QA).

**Method:** `pnpm --filter @lh/web exec playwright test --grep @visual` (spec: `apps/web/tests/e2e/visual.spec.ts`).

**What it covers:**
- 38 screens × {360×780, 1440×900} × {vi, en} = 152 runs.
- All screens use the mocked backend from `tests/e2e/fixtures.ts`. The fixture family is "Nhà họ Nguyễn": 2 narrators, 4 stories (including one experience card), 4 public items.

**Where the screenshots go:** `apps/web/test-results/visual/` (gitignored). Each screen produces:
- `<screen>.<width>.<locale>.png`: full page.
- `.fold.png`: above the fold.
- `.json`: the automated findings.

**Automated checks:**
- Horizontal overflow, with the leaf-most offending elements listed.
- Clipped headings: `overflow: hidden` or line-clamp, combined with scrollHeight > clientHeight.
- Display headings whose line-height is below 1.1. Stacked Vietnamese diacritics (ỗ, ẫ, Ữ) start to collide below that.
- Fixed/sticky bars overlapping each other.

**Status:**
- All routes in the brief exist and render. That includes the phase-3 `/cai-dat`, `/gia-dinh/du-lieu`, `/gia-dinh/album` and `/gia-dinh/cay-gia-dinh`.
- `/sach/:id` (builder + preview) and `/sach/:id/in` (print) were covered.
- No route needed `fixme`.

Priorities:
- **P0**: broken or unusable.
- **P1**: clearly wrong or ugly.
- **P2**: polish.

## P0: broken

### 1. The public header overflows at 360px. In English the menu button is pushed off-screen.
- **Where:** every `SiteLayout` page (landing, auth, legal, `/kham-pha`, `/tri-thuc/:id`, `/da-luu`).
- **Measured:** 2px overflow in vi, 39px in en. The en hamburger sits at x=383 on a 360px screen, so mobile English visitors cannot reach the navigation.
- **Root cause:** `cn()` (`src/lib/cn.ts`) only joins classes; it does not merge them. `SiteHeader.tsx` passes `className="hidden sm:inline-flex"` to `<LanguageSwitch>`, but the switch's own `inline-flex` still applies, so the switch never hides below `sm`. The same pattern can silently break any component that accepts a `className` override.
- **Fix, two parts:**
  - Let `LanguageSwitch` put the caller's display class first, or wrap it in a `<div className="hidden sm:block">`.
  - Make the mobile language choice available inside the slide-down menu, which already exists.
- **Longer term:** add a tiny class-merge for conflicting display utilities, or document that `className` must not fight base display classes.
- **Test status:** `landing.spec.ts › no horizontal scroll at 360px` is marked `test.fail` until this is fixed. Remove that line once it is.

### 2. The NarratorShell header overflows at 360px in English: the EN button is off-screen.
- **Where:** `/ke-chuyen`, `/ke-chuyen/cua-toi`, `/ke-chuyen/phien/:id`.
- **Measured:** 40px overflow in en, 2px in vi.
- **What the narrator sees:**
  - The logo wraps to two lines ("Di Sản / Sống", "Living / Heritage").
  - The language toggle is partly hidden.
- This is the elderly-narrator screen, so it is the worst place for this bug.
- **File:** `components/layout/layouts.tsx` (`NarratorShell`). Options:
  - Show only `LogoMark` below `sm`.
  - Or move `TextScaleSwitch` + `LanguageSwitch` into one compact "Aa · VI/EN" popover.
  - Give the header `min-w-0` and `shrink-0` on the controls.

### 3. Members page: cards are cut off on the right at 360px.
- **Where:** `/gia-dinh/thanh-vien`.
- **Measured:** 81px overflow in vi, 98px in en.
- **What is hidden:** the card's right edge, the role `<select>`, the delete button, and the invite form inputs.
- **File:** `features/families/MembersPage.tsx`. The outer `grid gap-6 lg:grid-cols-[…]` has no explicit column on mobile, so the implicit track grows to the min-content of a wide child.
- **Fix:** add `grid-cols-1` (minmax(0, 1fr)) or `min-w-0` on the grid children.

### 4. Narrators page: same overflow at 360px.
- **Where:** `/gia-dinh/nguoi-ke`.
- **Measured:** 81px overflow in vi, 105px in en.
- **What is cut:** the "Tạo liên kết kể chuyện" and "Kể cùng … trên thiết bị này" buttons, plus the edit icon.
- **File:** `features/narrators/NarratorsPage.tsx`, the `ul.grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2` at line 127.
- **Fix:** add `grid-cols-1`, and allow buttons to wrap (`whitespace-normal`, `text-balance`).

## P1: wrong or ugly

### 5. The review page's sticky approval bar covers a third (desktop) to a half (mobile) of the screen.
- **Where:** `/ban-nhap/:id`.
- **Desktop (1440×900):** the bar is about 290px tall and covers the transcript and editor while you read.
- **Mobile (360):** the bar covers the "Nguồn lời kể / Bản nháp" tab switcher. On the narrator view the bar is "Lưu bản nháp" + question + "Xác nhận đúng ý tôi", which is about 340 of 780px.
- **Files:** `features/review/ReviewPage.tsx` and `ApprovalBar.tsx` (the `sticky bottom-0` wrapper). Options:
  - Keep only a slim sticky row: save state + primary button.
  - Move the assisted-consent explainer and checkbox into the page flow above it.
  - On mobile, make the bar non-sticky once the user scrolls to the end.
- **Experience drafts:** the bar's left half is empty while the right column runs on (`review-experience.1440`).

### 6. After approval, the same "Chọn nơi lưu và chia sẻ" CTA appears twice.
- One copy is in the success status panel (`ApprovalBar.tsx:63`); the other is in `ReviewPage.tsx:357`.
- **Fix:** keep one.
- The e2e test scopes its click to the status panel to cope with this.

### 7. The not-found state on `/tri-thuc/:id` has no h1.
- `EmptyState` renders "Không tìm thấy nội dung" as an h3, so the page has no h1 at all.
- **Files:** `features/knowledge/pages/ItemPage.tsx` plus `components/ui/EmptyState.tsx`.
- **Fix:** add a `headingLevel` prop, or let pages pass `as="h1"` for whole-page states. Check the other whole-page empty and error states for the same thing.

### 8. The print view `/sach/:id/in` has no h1.
- The book title is a styled div/h2 on the cover.
- **File:** `features/books/BookPrint.tsx`.
- **Fix:** use an `h1` for the cover title (print CSS is unaffected).
- **Test status:** a11y-lite marks this route `test.fail` (`routes.ts → knownA11yIssue`).

### 9. The mobile tab bar truncates labels.
- **Examples:** "Khám phá t…", "Explore…", "Memory…".
- **File:** `components/layout/layouts.tsx` (AppShell mobile nav, `text-[11px] line-clamp-1`).
- **Fix:** add short nav labels to common (`nav.short.*`): "Khám phá", "Sách", "Explore", "Books". Don't truncate.

### 10. The landing hero headline line-height is 1.08–1.10.
- **File:** `components/landing/Hero.tsx`.
- **Effect:** the stacked marks in "Giữ lại giọng kể" ("ữ", "ể") nearly touch the line above at 360px.
- Other display h1s, in StaticPage and auth, use 1.12, which is borderline.
- **Fix:** set display headings to ≥ 1.15 for vi. Use `:lang(vi)` or a token like `--leading-display-vi`.

## Design critique vs the remento.co / storycorps.org bar

**What is already at that level:**
- **Landing.** Editorial serif + sans pairing, warm paper palette and confident whitespace. The hero carousel, the giant "Di Sản Sống" manifesto block and the "4 bước" cards read as a crafted brand, not a template.
- **Story detail** (topic-coloured hero band + illustration) and **item detail** (topic wash + scene). They feel like StoryCorps' archive pages, and better than most family-memory apps.
- **Auth.** The split layout with the night-house illustration and the proverb is warm and on-brand.
- **Narrator mode.** Big type, one primary action per screen, a calm how-to sheet. This is right for elderly users.

**Where it falls short:**
1. **Mobile is the weakest surface**, even though families and narrators will mostly use phones. It has all four P0s plus the review sticky bar. Remento's whole product is mobile-first.
   - The rule should be: every page passes at 360px in English. English labels run 20–40% longer than Vietnamese ones here.
   - Run `--grep @visual` before merging UI work.
2. **The signed-in app is visually flatter than the marketing site.**
   - Vault, members, narrators, books, settings and data-center are all the same pattern: cream page, white rounded card, serif h2, grey text.
   - There is no imagery, no narrator avatars (only initials or a generic icon), and no topic colour in the vault list, even though the public hub has a lovely topic palette (`features/knowledge/topics.ts → topicStyle`).
   - Fixes:
     - Reuse the topic band and scene in vault story cards and in the review header.
     - Use narrator portraits or illustrated avatars where available.
3. **Hierarchy on the work screens.**
   - The review page gives equal weight to the source panel, the editor and a heavy sticky footer.
   - StoryCorps-style clarity would be:
     - The story text is the hero.
     - The transcript sits in a quieter side rail.
     - The approval is a single, obvious, final step.
4. **Spacing scale drifts.**
   - Page tops vary: about 40px (vault and settings h1 sit close to the top) versus about 110px (books, collections, story detail with a back link).
   - Header action clusters are aligned differently on each page (vault: right of the h1; books: under it, centred; collections: floating right).
   - Fix: use one `PageHeader` (eyebrow, h1, lede, actions) for every AppShell page.
5. **Empty states.**
   - The vault empty state is good: icon, one sentence, one action.
   - Album, collections and admin empties are bare boxes. Give each a one-line "why this matters" and the single next action, matching B16.
6. **Vietnamese typography.**
   - Diacritics render correctly everywhere. No clipping was detected by the overflow/line-clamp scan.
   - The risk is tight display leading (P1 #10).
   - The handwritten Caveat accents ("Chỉ cần nói thôi") appear to lack Vietnamese glyph coverage for some stacked marks (verify against the font subset). "kể" and "truyền" fall back mid-word in the manifesto ("* Giữ lại giọng kể, trao truyền kinh nghiệm" mixes glyph styles).
   - Fix: use a Vietnamese-complete hand font, or limit Caveat to short ASCII-safe notes.
